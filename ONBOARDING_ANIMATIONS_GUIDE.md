## 💡 Tips

### **Animation Performance:**
- Keep JSON files under 500KB
- Use simple animations for mobile
- Test on real devices

### **Design Consistency:**
- All animations should match the dark theme
- Use similar color palettes
- Keep animation duration 2-4 seconds

### **User Experience:**
- Animations should enhance, not distract
- Keep them subtle and professional
- Ensure they loop smoothly
